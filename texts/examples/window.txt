# A console window of its own, moved around the screen
set the title to Brick Window Demo
put the window on the left side of the screen
clear the screen
write in green ten in the middle of the screen
wait 2 seconds
put the window on the right side of the screen
clear the screen
write "now on the right" in yellow in the middle of the screen
wait 2 seconds
make the window smaller, centre the window
clear the screen
write "smaller, in the middle; the console is" the width of the console "columns wide" at the top
