# brick's textual universe: what words are about, and what those ideas are about in turn.
#
# Words to an idea:          idea: word, word, a phrase, ...
# An idea to a wider idea:   idea is a wider idea
#
# brick only asks the universe about a line it can't read by itself. The universe works out what each
# word is about from the words around it (pink is a colour, a colour is a look, a look belongs to the
# thing being shown), then says the line again in brick's own words.
# Colours, and the dictionary's words for writing, waiting and so on, join their ideas by themselves:
# crimson is a colour, and scribble means write, so scribble is about showing.

# What a line can be about
show: write, say, show, print, display, output, type, tell, echo, pen, pop up
placing: put, place, stick, post, slap, plop, move
make: make, turn, set, change, paint
clear: clear, wipe, erase, cls, empty, clean
wait: wait, pause, sleep, hold on, hang on, hold

# Things
text: text, words, word, message, msg, note, label, writing, letters, txt, sign, caption
window: window
background: background, bg, backdrop, backround, bkg
screen: screen, console, terminal, scr

# Where things go
place: left, right, top, bottom, middle, centre, center, top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right, upper, lower, corner, side, edge, half
toward: at, on, to, in, near, towards, toward, by, onto, into

# How things look
look word: colour, color, colours, colors, coloured, colored, shade, tint
style: bold, italic, italics, underlined, underline
by means of: in, with, using
becoming: to, into

# The words to show
saying: saying, says, that says, which says, it says, and it says, reading, reads, that reads, which reads, the words, the word, with the words, with the text, make it say, have it say, let it say, and make it say, make it read, so it says, to say

# Time and counting
time unit: second, seconds, sec, secs, s, minute, minutes, min, mins, millisecond, milliseconds, ms, hour, hours, hr, hrs
later: after, in
times: times, time

# How big the window is
size: bigger, smaller, wider, taller, narrower, shorter, full screen, fullscreen

# Words that don't change what a line means
filler: can you, could you, would you, will you, can u, could u, please, pls, plz, kindly, go ahead, for me, i want, i need, i want you to, real quick
# Chatter: only left out at the start or end of a direction ('um write hi', 'write hi lol'); in the middle it's words
chatter: um, uh, erm, so, so yeah, yeah, yep, ok, ok so, okay, now, hey, yo, lol, thx, thanks, rn, asap, also, just, quickly, quick, anyway, ya
it: it, that, this
glue: the, a, an, of, and
